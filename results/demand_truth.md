## (b) Energy balance

### Sign convention

- 5,161,777 of 5,173,352 hours with raw demand (99.8%) also have net generation and interchange.
- In those hours demand is within 1 MW of net generation minus interchange in 81.2% of hours, and of net generation plus interchange in 0.7%.
- Per BA, over hours with interchange beyond 1 MW, the plus sign fits better in 0 of 58 BAs. So positive interchange is a net export, and demand = net generation − interchange.

## Proposal

### Truth series

- Raw demand ("Demand (MW)") is the truth.
- R4: drop the hours EIA changed (raw reported and imputed set). Adjusted equals raw everywhere else, so these are exactly the hours where EIA's series disagrees with the raw one. Of its 10,001 hours, 9,474 are negative values that R1 already drops; 513 remain after R1-R3a.
- Robustness check: EIA's adjusted demand as the truth.

### Demand-validity rule R5

- Drop an hour when demand differs from net generation minus interchange by more than 10% of demand and more than 1 MW.
- Keep hours missing net generation or interchange: the balance cannot be checked there, and missing is not wrong (10,146 usable hours).
- 10% is the go/no-go check's upper bound for a plausible forecast MAPE. A demand value that disagrees with its own components by more than that cannot judge forecast errors of that size.

Threshold sensitivity. Hours removed are counted after R1, R2, R3a and R4.
